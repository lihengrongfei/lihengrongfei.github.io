About

Robotics, perception, and control.

I am a master’s student in Electrical Engineering and Information Technology at TUM, specializing in automation and robotics. My work focuses on developing intelligent robotic systems that connect perception, control, and learning with reliable performance in simulation and on real hardware.

My current interests include physical and embodied AI, robot teleoperation, model-based control, computer vision, and deep learning. I am particularly interested in applying these methods to robotic manipulation and human–robot interaction.

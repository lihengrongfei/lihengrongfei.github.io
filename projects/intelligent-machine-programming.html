Robot Programming & Manipulation

Intelligent Machine Programming Lab

A sequence of practical assignments covering offline robot programming, physical interaction, kinesthetic teaching, and constrained motion control.

Apr — Jun 2026RoboDK · ROS 2 · Franka · UR5e
Course repositories ↗

Overview

The lab connects simulation and real robot hardware through four focused assignments: converting vector graphics into robot motion, force-aware interaction, recording and replaying taught trajectories, and Remote Center of Motion control for a surgical-tool model.

Highlights

  • Offline programming and path generation
  • Physical manipulation with Franka and UR robots
  • Task-priority constrained control in ROS 2
